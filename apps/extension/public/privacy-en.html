Privacy policy — PrivatePrompt Guard

By AI4Context · Version 0.1 · Updated: 16 August 2026

PrivatePrompt Guard is a Chrome extension that reviews the text in the message box just before you send on ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini web, to flag possible sensitive data. Analysis runs on your device.

What is processed

  • Composer text on the AI page, only when you try to send.
  • Preferences (enabled, sensitivity, language, Nano assist) in chrome.storage.local.

What we do not do

  • We do not send your prompt content to AI4Context servers.
  • We do not monitor tabs in the background or read unrelated sites.
  • We do not sell data or run ads based on your prompts.

Permissions

The extension only requests access to ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini domains needed to read the composer and intercept send, plus local storage and the active tab when you open the popup.

Optional on-device AI

If you enable Gemini Nano assist (Chrome Prompt API) and it is available, some fragments may be classified on-device. If Nano is unavailable, only deterministic rules run.

Limitations

This is not enterprise DLP and may miss secrets. You decide whether to send after the warning.
